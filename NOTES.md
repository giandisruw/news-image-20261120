### 1. ICJ: Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya (LEAD)
- Thu 19 Nov, The Hague: the International Court of Justice ruled Myanmar
  committed genocide against its Rohingya minority — the first time in
  the court's 80-year history it has held a state responsible for genocide.
- Vote 9–6 that the military's 2017 "clearance operations" in Rakhine state
  were carried out with intent to destroy a substantial part of the group.
- The court put the violently killed at about 10,000 (including up to 2,109
  children under 5) and ordered Myanmar to pay reparations, allow safe,
  voluntary and dignified return, and report back within two years.
- Case brought by The Gambia in Nov 2019 under the 1948 Genocide Convention;
  judgment final, binding, no appeal; the court has no enforcement mechanism.
- Sources: UN News (19 Nov), CNN (19 Nov), Le Monde (19/20 Nov), NPR via
  WEXT Radio (19 Nov), Amnesty International (19 Nov).

- Sources: Reuters via SRN News (19 Nov), NY Post (19 Nov), Axios via
  Internewscast (19 Nov), Breitbart (18/19 Nov).

### 3. Three leaders sign "Declaration toward Peace" in Shenzhen
- Thu 19 Nov: first-ever trilateral of Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir
  Putin, ~90 minutes at Kylin Villa, Shenzhen, on the APEC sidelines.
- Four-point "Declaration toward Peace": a nuclear war "cannot be won and
  must never be fought", dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing
  proliferation, more refined petroleum output and stable energy flows, UN
  reform, and that the ICC must not abuse its jurisdiction.
- No dates, no figures, no timetable, and no commitment on Ukraine; Putin
  said afterwards Russia "will not negotiate" on ceasefires after Ukraine's
  Crimean Bridge strikes. Trump invited Putin to the G20 in Miami in Dec.
- Sources: USA Today (19 Nov), Bernama via PJM TV (20 Nov), MarketFlux
  (19 Nov), JagranJosh (19 Nov), astig.ph (19 Nov).

### 4. Cyclone Akvan due at noon today
- Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan over the Bay of Bengal, due to cross the
  north Andhra Pradesh–Yanam and south Odisha coasts between Narsapur and
  Puri, close to the east of Gopalpur, around noon IST Fri 20 Nov
  (06:30 UTC), with sustained winds 90–100 km/h gusting to 110 km/h.
- The Hindu's live blog (20 Nov 01:43) reports three lakh (300,000) people
  evacuated and 423 rescue teams deployed; an earlier Odisha figure was
  ~72,000 evacuated. Red alerts for six Odisha districts; 20–30 cm of rain
  expected. Third cyclone on this coast in a month (after Arnab, Murasu).
- Sources: India Meteorological Department bulletins (19 Nov), The Hindu
  live blog (19–20 Nov), JagranJosh (19 Nov), Sambad English (19 Nov),
  Zenitmap (19 Nov).

### 5. Houthi missile hits Riyadh's tallest tower
- Thu 19 Nov, early hours: a suspected Houthi missile struck the PIF Tower
  in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District — the 80-storey, 385 m
  headquarters of Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund (PIF, $900bn+ assets).
  Windows blown out across seven to eight floors, metal facade bent; no
  injuries reported; financial district closed to visitors.
- Saudi-led coalition said it "dealt with" a missile threat towards Riyadh;
  the Houthis claimed cruise-missile attacks on Riyadh airport and the
  financial district. Second strike on the district in 11 days (Aramco
  building hit 8 Nov).
- Sources: Reuters via Sowetan (19 Nov), AFP via The Library Estate
  (19 Nov), The Curtain (19 Nov), Times Now World (19 Nov).
